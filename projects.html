Foresight

Full-stack market intelligence and portfolio simulation dashboard that turns live market data into scenario forecasts, allocation guidance, and finance-learning workflows.

  • Trained a hierarchical RL system: 3 PPO sub-agents (stocks, crypto, ETFs) coordinated by a SAC meta-agent; reward functions penalise concentration and reward benchmark alpha.
  • Added surrogate-SHAP explainability to surface per-sleeve allocation decisions; risk-based cash caps prevent degenerate policies.
  • Built a 55-asset market universe on Supabase with automated refresh across OHLCV, macro context, and 30/90/180/300-day forecast snapshots.
  • Shipped 13 FastAPI routes, CI/CD via GitHub Actions, and a $10K portfolio simulator with bear/base/bull paths up to 730 days, deployed on Render.

Browser-based Formula 1 analytics dashboard covering 27 seasons, with live current-season data, race drill-downs, and a grounded F1 chat assistant.

  • Six interactive charts plus per-race drill-downs with qualifying tables, top-10 results, and grid-to-finish slope charts
